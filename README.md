# ShopLink

**Hyper-local inventory exchange network for neighborhood shops**

ShopLink connects independent local shops (kirana, hardware, general stores and similar) so they can share excess and near-expiry stock within their neighborhood — reducing dead stock losses and stock-outs without depending on large marketplaces or wholesalers.

---

## Overview

Small retail shops frequently face two opposing problems at the same time:

- Excess or near-expiry inventory sitting idle (dead stock)
- Sudden stock-outs of the same products in nearby shops

ShopLink creates a trusted, location-aware network that lets shops discover, request, and exchange inventory with neighboring businesses in real time. The platform focuses on hyper-local matching, simple workflows for non-technical shopkeepers, and secure transaction handling.

The long-term vision is to become the default inventory-sharing layer for independent retail across towns and cities.

---

## Core Capabilities

- Neighborhood shop discovery and matching
- Dead-stock and excess inventory listing
- Real-time availability and request flow
- Secure transaction and settlement layer
- Order tracking and delivery options (pickup / local delivery)
- Seller analytics and loss-recovery insights
- Multi-language support focused on Indian retail
- Admin tools for verification, moderation and platform health

---

## Who It’s For

- Independent kirana and general stores
- Hardware and local specialty shops
- Small multi-outlet retailers looking to optimize inventory across nearby locations
- Future partners (brands, distributors, logistics providers)

---

## Product Direction

ShopLink is being built as a scalable B2B platform with strong network effects. Early focus is on dense local clusters, high match rates, and trust systems that encourage repeated use. Over time the platform will expand into deeper inventory intelligence, partnerships, and multi-city coverage.

---

## Status

Active development. Core flows and infrastructure are in progress. Private beta and early shop onboarding are planned for selected localities.
